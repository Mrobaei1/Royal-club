**Decision date:** 2026-11-03  
**Scope:** `standalone/hokm-lab/` on `research/hokm-standalone` only.  
**Integration boundary:** This work remains isolated. Do not merge into `main`, register it in the production app/API, deploy it, or build an APK without a separate explicit owner instruction.

This document converts the owner's Hokm questionnaire decisions into implementation and acceptance requirements. A selected multiple-choice answer is not automatically accepted when it conflicts with security or a later explicit clarification; those cases are recorded below rather than silently implemented.

## 1. Non-negotiable product boundary

- Royal Club is a game platform, not an audio/video calling or conferencing application.
- **Voice calls, video calls, conference calls, live push-to-talk, WebRTC calling/signalling, TURN/coturn, media relays, and equivalent live-call infrastructure are out of scope and must not be implemented.**
- Hokm may support ordinary text chat and the already-approved chat message types within the platform's communication policy.
- Recorded voice messages are distinct from calls. They remain future-optional, dormant, and disabled; no user-facing microphone/record/send/play action is enabled in this work. Any future activation requires a separate owner decision and security/cost review. Do not provision paid media services.
- Keep Hokm independent under `standalone/hokm-lab/` and `research/hokm-standalone`; no production integration is authorized by this specification.

## 2. Match, room, and scoring rules

1. A match contains four human seats, two teams, with partners in opposite seats.
2. The manager chooses the match target from **3, 5, or 7 hand points**.
3. The first team to reach the configured target wins immediately. A match is never a draw.
4. Offline/practice mode is excluded from this approved Hokm product scope.
5. A room is cleaned up/expires only when it is empty. Elapsed time alone must never expire an occupied room.
6. The server is authoritative for cards, legal actions, score, match completion, and final result.
7. The result is committed to the server before player progression/ranking is updated.
8. On an unrecoverable server/technical failure that makes the match impossible to continue, cancel the match; do not declare either team the winner and do not record a win/loss.
9. A player may file an appeal against a completed result. The system must preserve the result and make the appeal and match audit information available for management review; an appeal must not silently rewrite a result.

## 3. Turn deadlines and automatic action

- Trump selection deadline: **7 seconds**.
- Card-play deadline: **7 seconds**.
- If the active player does not play a card before the deadline, the server selects a card uniformly/randomly from that player's **currently legal cards only**. The normal server-side legal-move validator must still be used.
- If the hakem does not choose trump before the 7-second deadline, the fixed Hard Bot policy makes the selection from the hakem's own hand and public information only.
- Deadline processing is server-side, version-checked, race-safe, and idempotent. Clients cannot submit or override server timestamps or automatic actions.
- Duplicate/stale requests must never execute the same move twice. Use expected room versions and a unique operation/idempotency identifier at the transport/persistence boundary. Do not execute duplicate moves merely because two requests arrived.

## 4. End-of-hand reveal and privacy

- During an active hand, each player receives only their own private hand plus public game information. Opponent hands and the undealt deck must never be sent to clients.
- At the end of **each hand**, reveal the cards still remaining in all four hands to all four players for exactly **3 seconds**.
- After the 3-second reveal, proceed to the next hand if the match has not ended.
- The reveal is a short end-of-hand disclosure, not a match replay.
- After the entire match, player-facing history is limited to the final result; do not expose a full replay/history viewer. Normal public information needed during an active hand remains available.
- All views are generated by the server and must not depend on client-side hiding of secret data.

## 5. Disconnect, application exit, and bot takeover

- A transient network disconnect receives a **20-second reconnect grace period** by default. It has no XP penalty.
- When that grace expires, the fixed Hard Bot temporarily takes over the disconnected seat and acts only through the normal legal-action validator.
- If the authenticated player reconnects after temporary takeover, they may reclaim their seat. Already committed bot moves remain; never roll them back.
- If all four players are simultaneously disconnected, preserve the last valid match state for **20 seconds**. If none reconnects before the deadline, cancel the match.
- A deliberate active-match forfeit is different from transient network loss: apply the existing **−5 XP once** to that player, transfer the seat to the fixed Hard Bot permanently for that match, and do not allow that player to reclaim the seat.
- If an app is closed/backgrounded while the player has not explicitly forfeited, treat it as a connectivity/presence event, not as proof of intentional forfeit. The 7-second turn deadline and legal automatic action still apply.
- A player who has forfeited cannot send further game commands.
- All disconnect/reconnect/timeout/forfeit transitions must use authenticated identity, expected room version, and race-safe persistence.

## 6. Match cancellation and communication

- A player cannot unilaterally cancel an active match.
- Cancellation by player agreement occurs only after **all four players** have explicitly requested/confirmed cancellation. One dissenting or missing vote means the match continues.
- Private text chat with players on the opposing team is allowed during the match.
- Chat permission does not expose hidden cards or alter game state. Chat messages must remain outside the game-command authorization path.
- A player may report suspected cheating (including disclosure of their cards to an opponent). The system must support a report and management review; it must not automatically award the match to either team based solely on an accusation.

## 7. Security, consistency, and result integrity

- Server-authoritative state is the sole source of truth. Client screenshots, local score, majority display, or a player's device state cannot override it.
- The server validates every command, turn, seat, card ownership, follow-suit rule, deadline, and expected version.
- Stale/replayed/duplicate commands are rejected or safely acknowledged as already processed; they must not apply twice.
- Private hands are delivered only to the corresponding authenticated seat. The server retains the full deal privately.
- Final match result and XP/level updates are committed transactionally or through an idempotent ledger/outbox pattern.
- Level progression remains monotonic; no level loss.
- XP policy already present in the isolated lab remains: after the whole match, each winning-team player receives +20 XP, each losing-team player +5 XP; an intentional active-match leaver receives −5 XP once; XP never falls below zero. Offline/practice XP is not applicable because practice mode is excluded.
- The fixed Hard Bot cannot be selected or downgraded by a player or room setting.

### Technical reconciliation notes

- The literal response to Q28 conflicts with duplicate-move safety. The implementation must prevent duplicate execution; this is a correctness/security invariant, not a cosmetic preference.

## 9. Implementation status after owner-decision work (2026-11-03)

The isolated lab already has a rules engine, match target 3/5/7, four-seat rooms, server-authoritative private seat views, fixed Hard Bot, XP progression, forfeit handling, temporary disconnect/reconnect, 20-second default per-player disconnect grace, PostgreSQL adapter foundation, and tests.

### Implemented in the room state machine and service boundary

1. **Implemented:** seven-second server turn deadlines; expired trump selection uses the fixed Hard policy; expired card play chooses randomly from legal cards only. A trusted service method processes the deadline with expected-version checks.
2. **Implemented:** three-second post-hand reveal of all remaining hands; the next hand cannot start before the reveal window expires.
3. **Implemented:** twenty-second all-four-disconnected deadline and cancellation transition, including the round-completed state. A trusted service method processes the deadline.
4. **Implemented:** unanimous four-player cancellation votes; fewer than four approvals cannot cancel.
5. **Implemented:** unrecoverable technical-failure cancellation with no winner/result and no progression award.
6. **Implemented:** participant appeal records and manager/owner review records. Review does not silently rewrite the committed result.
7. **Implemented:** participant cheating reports and manager/owner review records; a report does not automatically adjudicate the match.
8. **Implemented:** completed-match player view returns no full game/replay history; the short end-of-hand reveal remains available for its three-second window.
9. **Implemented at service boundary:** authenticated service methods exist for the above operations; trusted scheduler and manager methods reject ordinary player principals.
